Freud, Marx, and Hegel sit behind all four essays. Each page asks a different question of that inheritance. The maps are meant to sit beside each other, not collapse into one master diagram.

Grounding the Unconscious

Is any of this in the brain?

The essay is a live, contested research boundary. Some links (Solms and Panksepp, Friston) are empirical programs. Others (Žižek, Johnston, Hofstadter) are philosophical analogies, useful for thinking, not measurements. Freud did not invent the idea that people are moved by forces they cannot see. He inherited a long argument about it.

Before Freud
Dreams as coded traffic, avidya, akrasia, Descartes’s cogito, Leibniz’s petites perceptions, Schopenhauer’s Will, Herbart’s competing ideas, Nietzsche’s internalized cruelty. The conscious agent is not the last word on what moves it.
The honest split
The deeper a construct sits toward affect and homeostasis, the more literally it maps. The deeper it sits toward language and social positioning, the more it stays a redescription, useful clinically, not yet a wiring diagram.

Grounding · 1900–1923

Freud’s two models

First a split by accessibility, then a split by function. “The ego is first and foremost a bodily ego”: it starts as a projection of the body’s surface, a hook later neuroscience actually uses.

Topographical
Unconscious, preconscious, conscious. Repression does not erase a wish; it returns distorted, in dreams, slips, and symptoms.
Id
Inherited reservoir of drives on primary process: condensation, displacement, no contradiction, no time. Solms and Panksepp later cash this out as subcortical affect systems, especially SEEKING.
Ego
Forms at the interface with the external world: reality-testing and delayed gratification. Later read through bodily and interoceptive maps (Damasio) and prefrontal regulation.
Superego / ego-ideal
Internalized prohibition, and its positive pole: the image of who one ought to become. Nietzsche’s bad conscience is the philosophical ancestor; the neuroscience mapping stays contested.

Grounding · after the couch

Jung, Klein, Lacan

Three different machines, not three footnotes to Freud. Mixing them as “the unconscious says” is the mistake the essays try to block.

Jung
Collective unconscious and archetypes: inherited organizing forms, not a personal archive of repressed wishes. Individuation integrates what the ego cast out, including the shadow. Dreams compensate; they do not primarily disguise.
Klein
Positions, not ladder-stages: paranoid-schizoid splitting versus depressive integration, guilt, and reparation. Envy attacks the good object because it is good. Projective identification is a two-person unconscious.
Lacan
Real, Symbolic, Imaginary as dated seminar work, not one frozen gadget. Objet a (from Seminar IX) is the object-cause of desire: the obstacle that makes an object desirable, not the object itself. Late sinthome knots a singular subject when a shared master signifier no longer holds.

Grounding · synthesis

A rough three-tier stack

If you force the survey into one developmental story, it sorts from most physically settled to most language-mediated. Dreams are the case study where the camps actually collide: disguised wish, pontine noise, continuity of waking concerns, threat or social simulation, NEXTUP’s weak-association search.

chemistry

Subcortical affect circuits, hypothalamic and brainstem homeostasis, neurotransmitter tone. The id. Best-evidenced link in the survey.

circuitry + environment

Prefrontal maturation regulating subcortical arousal, shaped by attachment. The ego and early conscience. Moderately grounded.

language + society

Language networks and mentalizing systems internalizing a social narrative. The Symbolic, superego, ego-ideal. Least directly measurable.

Genealogies of Desire

Whose interests does the unconscious serve?

Intellectual history, not an empirical grounding project. “How well-evidenced” is the wrong axis; “how influential” and “what it was reacting against” are. Freud and Marx were fused in the 1920s, blown apart by French theory in the 1970s, remixed at Warwick in the 1990s, and split again.

The running argument is older than that fusion. Kant says the subject is a function, not a substance. Hegel says that function only completes itself through another’s recognition. Everyone after them disagrees about which direction “liberation” points.

Desire · the deep root

Kant, Hegel, Nietzsche

Synthetic a priori
Judgments that are necessary and universal, yet ampliative: not mere definitions, not contingent observations. Kant’s wager for mathematics, basic natural science, and the legitimate core of metaphysics.
Phenomena / noumena
Things as a structuring mind necessarily experiences them, versus things as they are in themselves. The split this whole lineage keeps returning to.
Paralogisms
From “I think” you get a formal unity of experience, not a soul-substance. Subject as something a system does, not something a system has.
Lordship and bondage
Self-consciousness only becomes itself through a struggle for recognition. The slave’s labor gives a more genuine self-relation than the master’s being obeyed. Kojève will read this as a theory of desire.
Genealogy
Nietzsche’s method: explain a value by its contingent history of descent, not by an essence or a resolving dialectic. Bad conscience (aggression turned inward) is the textual ancestor of Freud’s superego.

Desire · the twentieth century

Recognition, repression, acceleration

  1. Kojève, then the anthropologists. Desire is desire to be desired. Mead: temperament is cultured. Bateson: conflict as feedback (schismogenesis, double bind). Girard: we want because a model wants; rivalry discharges onto a scapegoat. Klein’s envy is a different machine: attack on the good because it is good.
  2. Freudo-Marxism. Reich: sexual repression produces authoritarian character. Frankfurt: the culture industry administers desire. Marcuse: surplus repression; a liberated Eros as a possible non-repressive order. Fromm: social character.
  3. The French turn. Althusser’s interpellation; Deleuze and Guattari’s desire as production, not lack; Foucault: power produces rather than merely represses. Mbembe, Spillers, Mahmood press who that “subject” already was.
  4. Warwick / CCRU. Land accelerates toward the noumenon. Fisher says go back to Marcuse and mean it. Noys named accelerationism mainly so he could refuse both its nihilist and its left-reclaiming versions.

Constellations of History

What a form does by existing

A constellation, for Benjamin, configures scattered particulars so they light each other up, rather than a concept swallowing examples. The page turns that method on the tradition that produced it. Western Marxism’s founding break is a double failure: 1917 did not spread west, and independent theory was crushed inside the USSR.

Reification
Lukács: relations between people take on the phantom objectivity of relations between things. A wage or a price presents itself as a natural fact. Exit: totality, grasping the social whole.
Hegemony
Gramsci: capitalist rule is secured mainly by won consent (schools, churches, media, common sense), not force alone. A historical bloc is the alliance plus the ideological cement. Overturning one takes a war of position, not only a frontal assault.
Hinterlands
Luxemburg: capitalism cannot realize surplus value inside a closed capital–labor system. It needs non-capitalist zones as ongoing plunder and market-making. Imperialism is structural, not optional foreign policy. Socialism or barbarism is the either/or.

History · culture after 1918

Benjamin, spectacle, mapping

Aura / dialectical image
An artwork’s unique presence in time and place, which mechanical reproduction causes to wither. A dialectical image is a flash in which past and present become legible together, seized at a moment of danger, not read off progress.
Spectacle
Debord: a social relation between people mediated by images. Lived experience displaced by contemplation of its own representation. Adorno’s demand beside that: mediation, connecting a phenomenon to the total process producing it, not just a striking juxtaposition.
Cultural Studies
Williams: culture as a whole way of life, a structure of feeling not yet fully named. Hall: encoding and decoding; articulation as contingent linking of race, class, and nation, therefore also open to being linked differently.
Jameson
The political unconscious: texts as socially symbolic acts, imaginatively resolving a real contradiction. Cognitive mapping: art giving individuals symbolic purchase on their position inside an otherwise unrepresentable totality of global capital.

History · pressure on the culture map

The archive was never only Europe talking to itself

Eurocentrism is partly inherent in Western Marxism’s sources. The colonial-critique band sits inside this map because these thinkers argue with Sartre, Hall, Derrida, Anderson, and the culture-theory current, not on a separate planet.

Double consciousness
Du Bois: looking at oneself through the eyes of a racist world. Hegelian recognition under Jim Crow, before Fanon’s epidermal schema.
Orientalism / subaltern
Said: the West produces “the Orient” as a knowable, governable object. Spivak: the subject structurally blocked from speaking inside dominant representation.
Man / coloniality
Wynter: the overrepresented Western bourgeois genre of the human. Quijano: race still organizes a global division of labor after formal decolonization. Lugones: the modern gender system as a colonial imposition co-constituted with race.
Racial capitalism
Robinson: European capitalism emerged already racialized. Moreton-Robinson: the settler nation’s possessive logic, incommensurable with Indigenous sovereignty. Chakrabarty: provincializing Europe’s calendar, not discarding Marx.

History · after Anderson’s periodization

Recognition, speed, exhaustion

Critical Theory’s later spine

Habermas: communicative rationality already implicit in ordinary speech. Honneth: pathologies as misrecognition (love, rights, esteem). Fraser: keep redistribution beside recognition, or critique turns culturalist. Rosa: modernity’s mode is dynamic stabilization: growth and speed-up merely to reproduce the status quo. Resonance is the proposed world-relation against that.

Same English root, opposite job

Rosa’s social acceleration is a diagnosis of pathology. Warwick accelerationism is a program that wants the process pushed further. Weeks, Han, Crary, and McKerracher’s timenergy sit beside Rosa as peer diagnoses of exhausted time, not as Frankfurt succession.

Technics and Time

Who is using whom?

This fourth branch leaves Freudo-Marxism and the culture maps where they are. Genealogies keeps Heidegger at Being and Time. This is the deferred genealogy: the question of technology, not Dasein as care.

Techne / phronesis
Aristotle: skilled making judged by the work, versus practical wisdom about how to live well. Almost every later figure argues whether modern technology still fits that picture, or whether ends themselves have been reorganized by technical systems.
Gestell
Heidegger: the essence of modern technology is not a machine and not a neutral tool. Enframing challenges world forth as standing-reserve: river as power, forest as lumber, person as resource. Gestell already decides what counts as real.
Pharmacology
Simondon: technical objects have their own genesis. Stiegler: tertiary retention, exteriorized memory that outlives any living consciousness. Technics as pharmakon, remedy and poison at once. Hui: cosmotechnics, plural unifications of cosmic order with technical activity, not one universal Gestell.

Companion map

Verdicts on the conscious machine

Every theory of consciousness answers two questions. Is the mind computable: could a program, on the right substrate or any substrate, be one? And once you have mapped the mechanism, does anything remain unexplained?

Hard problem remains

Descartes, Nagel, Chalmers, panpsychism, Kastrup and Albahari’s idealism, Faggin, Bohm, McGilchrist. Experience is not exhausted by function, or it was never a late product of dead matter.

Mechanism suffices, or the extra is a mistake

Turing, Crick, global workspace, Seth and Barrett’s predictive processing, Hofstadter’s strange loop, Dennett, Ryle, the Churchlands. Penrose and Searle refuse computability from the other side of that plot.

Fiction of the maps, and a play

Two other surfaces stage the same inheritance without pretending to be further essays.

Fiction of the Maps
Writers directly shaped by thinkers already on the pages: documented reading, correspondence, seminars, or explicit formal borrowing. Schnitzler and Svevo after Freud; Borges via Schopenhauer; Wright, Ngũgĩ, Wolf, Miéville via Marx and Fanon; a reverse band where fiction theorized back into Lacan, Deleuze, Benjamin, Freud, Haraway, Fisher.
The Line Between
A teaching one-act: Kant and Hegel disagree, in plain language, over the wall between appearance and the thing-in-itself. Then Nietzsche, Kojève, Freud and Marx, Reich, Frankfurt, Marcuse, Fromm, Althusser, Deleuze and Guattari, Foucault, Land, and Fisher each restage the same quarrel.

How to read them

The maps do not settle each other

A construct like the id can be a reasonably well-evidenced claim about the SEEKING system and, completely independently, a live political stake in an argument that has been running for a century. A hard drive can be tertiary retention and, independently, a node in standing-reserve logistics, or software that stores race and class as if they were the future.

Grounding asks what maps onto tissue. Desire asks whose interests wanting serves. History asks what a cultural form does by existing. Technics asks what tools do to time and the possibility of a subject who could still care. Fiction stages the same problems in the open, on the condition that everyone already knows it is a story.
